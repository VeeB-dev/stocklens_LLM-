# StockLence

### AI-Powered Investment Research and Learning Platform

StockLence is an AI-based platform that helps beginners understand stocks, companies, financial reports, and investment concepts in simple language.

## Problem

Many beginners find investing difficult because:

- Financial reports are complicated.
- Important financial information is spread across different websites.
- Stock news is difficult to understand.
- Beginners don't know which financial metrics to check.
- Most platforms only show data but don't explain it.
- General AI chatbots do not provide enough company-specific analysis.

## Our Solution

StockLence brings **financial data, AI analysis, stock research, news, and investment learning** into one platform.

Users can search for a company and get important information explained in simple language.

## Main Features

### 1. AI Stock Research
Get AI-generated information about a company, including:

- Company overview
- Business model
- Revenue and profit
- Financial ratios
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Risks
- Competitors
- Latest news

### 2. Financial Report Analyzer
Upload a company's financial report and let StockLence extract and explain important information such as:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Assets
- Liabilities
- Debt
- EPS
- Cash flow

### 3. AI News Analyzer
Collect and summarize financial news and explain:

- What happened
- Why it matters
- Possible impact on the company

### 4. Company Comparison
Compare two or more companies based on:

- Revenue
- Profit
- P/E
- ROE
- Debt
- EPS
- Market Capitalization
- Growth

### 5. AI Risk Analyzer
Understand the major risks related to a company or portfolio in simple language.

### 6. Portfolio Analyzer
Users can add or upload their portfolio and understand:

- Portfolio performance
- Sector allocation
- Diversification
- Concentration
- Major risks

### 7. Stock Screener
Find companies using filters such as:

- Market Cap
- P/E
- ROE
- EPS Growth
- Debt
- Dividend
- Sector

### 8. Learn Investing
An AI-powered learning section covering:

- Stock Market Basics
- Fundamental Analysis
- Technical Analysis
- Financial Statements
- Valuation
- Portfolio Management
- Risk Management

Users can also take quizzes and use flashcards.

### 9. AI Research Reports
Generate a detailed company research report containing:

- Company overview
- Financial analysis
- SWOT analysis
- Growth opportunities
- Risks
- Competitor comparison
- Future outlook

### 10. AI Chat With Annual Reports
Users can upload an annual report and ask questions about it.

The AI finds relevant information from the report and provides answers with references to the source.

## Project Goal

The main goal of StockLence is to make **stock research and financial learning easier for beginners** by combining real financial data with AI-powered explanations.

> StockLence is an educational and research platform. It does not provide direct buy or sell recommendations.
